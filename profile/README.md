# Houseful of Love

We run **positive sum experiments**: small, sincere bets on tools and ways of working where, for us to win, nobody
has to lose. We learn in the open, credit prior art loudly, write down what failed as carefully as what worked, and
play long games with long-term people.

## Experiments

- **voracious-studio** (public beta, coming soon): give it your footage and some context, get back an edited video.
  A Claude Code workspace for programmatic video editing that learns your taste, your gear and your style, project
  by project.
